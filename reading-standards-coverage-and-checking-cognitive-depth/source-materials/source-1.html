Reading Standards Coverage and Checking Cognitive Depth — Topic Prompt (Source)

Module: Reading Standards Coverage The Standards Coverage view shows how well your course's materials cover the standards you have mapped, including whether you cover each standard at the right cognitive depth. Like the Standards Library, this is a newer feature currently labeled "System in Development." What the coverage view shows. Each mapped standard appears as a row showing its code, its statement, its cognitive (Bloom's) level, counts of the aligned assignments, quiz items, and activities, and a Status. A headline summarizes the whole picture — for example, how many of the aligned standards are actually assessed (as a percentage) and how many have a cognitive-depth gap. The tiered status. A standard's Status is one of a small set of tiers rather than a numeric score: it may be not aligned (nothing is mapped to it), not addressed (mapped but nothing yet practices or assesses it), practiced only (it is practiced through an activity or assignment but not assessed by a quiz), or assessed (an assessment item covers it). The important distinction is practiced-only versus assessed. There are no percentage bars or numeric "depth scores." The cognitive-depth check. Because both standards and learning outcomes carry Bloom's levels, the view flags any standard whose coverage is shallower than the standard's own level demands — a "depth gap." This flag appears inline on the standard's row (a warning marker with a tooltip) and is summarized in the headline. It is part of the coverage view itself: there is no separate "run the check" button, no generated depth report, and no option to export the report as a spreadsheet or PDF. The required depth comes from the standard's own cognitive level, not from any program setting. Strengthening shallow coverage. When a standard is only practiced, unassessed, or flagged with a depth gap, you strengthen it by adding or upgrading materials that target it at the right cognitive level — for example, adding higher-Bloom quiz questions
