Reading Standards Coverage and Checking Cognitive Depth — Topic Prompt (Source)

Reading Standards Coverage The Standards Coverage view shows how well your course's materials cover the standards you have mapped, including whether you cover each standard at the right cognitive depth. Like the Standards Library, this is a newer feature currently labeled "System in Development." What the coverage view shows. Each mapped standard appears as a row showing its code, its statement, its cognitive (Bloom's) level, counts of the aligned assignments, quiz items, and activities, and a Status. A headline summarizes the whole picture — for example, how many of the aligned standards are actually assessed (as a percentage) and how many have a cognitive-depth gap. The tiered status. A standard's Status is one of a small set of tiers rather than a numeric score: it may be not aligned (nothing is mapped to it), not addressed (mapped to the course, but no assignment, quiz item, or activity is aligned to it yet), practiced only, or assessed. There are no percentage bars or numeric "depth scores." What counts as assessment versus practice. This distinction is precise and must be stated correctly. Assignments and quiz items both count as ASSESSMENT. Only activities count as PRACTICE. So a standard reaches "assessed" as soon as an aligned assignment or an aligned quiz item exists; a standard shows "practiced only" when the only thing aligned to it is an activity. Do not describe an assignment as practice, and do not tell the reader to add a quiz item in order to move an assignment-covered standard from practiced to assessed — it is already assessed. The cognitive-depth check reads LEARNING OUTCOMES, not materials. This is the second point that must be stated correctly. A standard carries a Bloom's level, and so do the learning outcomes aligned to it. PreseMaker compares the standard's Bloom's level against the highest Bloom's level among the learning outcomes aligned to that standard.
