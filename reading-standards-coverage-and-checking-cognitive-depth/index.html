Reading Standards Coverage and Checking Cognitive Depth

This module guides you through PreseMaker's Standards Coverage View — learning to interpret tiered status indicators, distinguish assessment from practice, and identify cognitive-depth mismatches so you can make confident, evidence-based improvements to your course design.
