Reading Standards Coverage and Checking Cognitive Depth

This module walks you through PreseMaker's Standards Coverage view — how to read its layout, interpret tiered status indicators, distinguish assessment from practice, and use the platform's cognitive-depth analysis to ensure your course materials fully and appropriately address each mapped standard.
