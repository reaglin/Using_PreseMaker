Reading Standards Coverage and Checking Cognitive Depth

This module guides instructors and course designers through PreseMaker's Standards Coverage view, showing how to interpret coverage reports, understand the tiered status system, distinguish assessment from practice activities, and verify that learning outcomes meet the cognitive depth demanded by mapped standards.
