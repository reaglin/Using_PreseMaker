Module Topics

Introduction to Interactive Activity Templates

An overview of PreseMaker's library of game-like interactive activity templates and their role in enhancing learner engagement. This topic establishes the purpose and value of using pre-built templates for instructional content.
  • What Are Interactive Activity Templates? — PreseMaker provides a library of pre-built, game-like interactive activity templates designed to transform instructional content into engaging learner experiences.
  • The Role of Templates in Learner Engagement — Interactive activity templates serve a deliberate instructional purpose: to increase learner engagement by presenting content in participatory, rather than passive, formats.
  • Overview of Available Template Types — PreseMaker's library includes several distinct template types, each suited to different instructional goals and content structures.
  • The Value of Using Pre-Built Templates — Pre-built templates provide a structured starting point that standardizes quality and accelerates content production for instructors and course designers.
  • How Templates Fit Within the Module Workflow — Interactive activity templates are one component of PreseMaker's broader content generation workflow, working alongside custom materials to produce a complete set of module artifacts.

Matching and Flashcard Activities

A focused look at how to use matching and flashcard templates built from key terms within PreseMaker. Learners explore how these activity types reinforce vocabulary and concept recognition.
  • Overview of Matching and Flashcard Templates in PreseMaker — PreseMaker provides built-in templates specifically designed for matching and flashcard activities, making it easy to generate structured practice materials directly from course content.
  • How Matching Activities Are Built from Key Terms — Matching activities in PreseMaker pair key terms with their corresponding definitions or descriptions, challenging learners to correctly connect related items.
  • How Flashcard Activities Are Structured — Flashcard activities present one side of a term-definition pair at a time, prompting the learner to recall the corresponding information before flipping to confirm.
  • Vocabulary and Concept Recognition as Learning Goals — Both matching and flashcard activities are specifically designed to reinforce vocabulary acquisition and concept recognition, two foundational skills for deeper content mastery.
  • Generating a Matching or Flashcard Activity in PreseMaker — The process of generating a matching or flashcard activity in PreseMaker follows a straightforward workflow within the interactive activity generator.
  • Locating Generated Activity Artifacts in the Module Index — After a matching or flashcard activity is created, PreseMaker stores it as a traceable artifact within the Module Index for easy retrieval.

Cloze Passages and Crossword Puzzles

An exploration of cloze passage and crossword puzzle templates as tools for contextual and word-level practice. This topic covers how key terms are used to automatically populate these activity formats.
  • What Are Cloze Passages? — A cloze passage is a reading activity in which specific words are removed from a text and learners must fill in the blanks from memory or context.
  • How Key Terms Populate Cloze Passages — PreseMaker uses the module's AI-selected key terms to decide which words to remove and replace with blanks in the generated passage.
  • Introduction to Crossword Puzzle Templates — Crossword puzzles in PreseMaker are word-level activities that challenge learners to recall key terms based on definitional or contextual clues.
  • How Key Terms Populate Crossword Puzzles — Similar to cloze passages, crossword puzzles are automatically built from the module's key terms, with each term serving as an answer entry in the grid.
  • Contextual vs. Word-Level Practice — Cloze passages and crossword puzzles serve complementary roles: cloze passages emphasize vocabulary in context, while crosswords focus on word-level recall and spelling.
  • Locating Generated Artifacts in the Module Index — Once a cloze passage or crossword puzzle is generated, PreseMaker saves the activity as an artifact accessible through the Module Index.

Generating Custom Materials

An introduction to PreseMaker's custom materials generator, which allows instructors to define and produce tailored content beyond the standard template library. Learners discover how to configure and generate instructor-specific artifacts.
  • What Is the Custom Materials Generator? — PreseMaker's custom materials generator is a dedicated tool that allows instructors to define and produce tailored content that goes beyond the standard template library.
  • Defining Your Custom Material Type — Before generating content, instructors must specify what type of custom material they want to produce, shaping how PreseMaker structures the output.
  • Configuring the Generator Settings — You describe the material you want and supply details such as topic focus, audience level, and content scope, and PreseMaker generates the custom HTML.
  • Generating the Custom Artifact — After configuration is complete, instructors initiate the generation process, and PreseMaker produces the custom material based on the defined parameters.
  • Locating Produced Artifacts in the Module Index — All custom materials generated within a module are stored and accessible through the Module Index, serving as a centralized repository for produced content.

Locating Artifacts in the Module Index

A practical guide to finding and managing all generated activities and materials through the Module Index. This topic ensures learners can reliably access and organize every artifact produced during the module.
  • What Is the Module Index? — The Module Index is a centralized hub within PreseMaker that catalogs every artifact generated during a module session.
  • Navigating to the Module Index — Reaching the Module Index requires only a few steps from anywhere within PreseMaker.
  • Identifying Different Artifact Types — The Module Index labels each artifact by type so learners can quickly distinguish between interactive activities and custom materials.
  • Opening and Reviewing Artifacts — Any artifact listed in the Module Index can be opened directly from the index for immediate review or use.
  • Organizing and Managing Artifacts — The Module Index provides basic organizational tools to help learners and instructors keep generated content structured and accessible.
  • Verifying That All Artifacts Are Captured — After generating activities and materials, learners should confirm that every produced artifact appears correctly in the Module Index.
