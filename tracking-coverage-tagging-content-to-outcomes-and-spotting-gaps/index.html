Using PreseMaker

Tracking Coverage: Tagging Content to Outcomes and Spotting Gaps

This module walks instructors and designers through the practice of tagging instructional content to defined learning outcomes within PreseMaker, using coverage views to surface curriculum gaps, and closing the alignment loop across planning, teaching, and assessment. Use the resources below to explore the module's topics and materials.

A reading that explains how PreseMaker assigns outcome tags to AI-generated items and how instructors tag items they create manually, helping learners understand the mechanics behind keeping every piece of content tied to a defined learning objective.

A reading that describes what a coverage view is, how tagged content aggregates into the report, and how to interpret the resulting map of outcomes to content items, enabling instructors and designers to use the report as an analytical tool for course review.

A reading that explains how to read a coverage report to detect learning outcomes with insufficient or missing content, and how to act on those findings, guiding instructors through the process of diagnosing and addressing curriculum gaps.
