Using PreseMaker

Tracking Coverage: Tagging Content to Outcomes and Spotting Gaps

This module walks you through PreseMaker's content-tagging system — from understanding how learning items are linked to outcomes, to building coverage views, identifying curriculum gaps, and closing the loop between planning, teaching, and assessment.
