Course Outcomes vs. Module Outcomes and the Alignment Map

This module explores the distinction between course-level and module-level student learning outcomes and shows how an alignment map makes those relationships visible. Work through the topics below to learn how to write, classify, link, and audit outcomes — and ensure cognitive depth is consistent across every level of your course design.
