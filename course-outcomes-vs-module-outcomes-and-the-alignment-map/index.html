Course Outcomes vs. Module Outcomes and the Alignment Map

This module explores the critical distinctions between course-level and module-level learning outcomes, and introduces the alignment map as a practical tool for coherent course design. Work through the resources below to understand how to write, classify, and connect outcomes across instructional levels — and how to identify gaps in cognitive depth and coverage.
