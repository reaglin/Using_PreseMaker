PreseMaker Module 2: Understanding the Course - Module - Topic Structure

Watch on YouTube →

Author / Site: Dr. Ron Eaglin's Web and Database Programming

Summary: This video explains the foundational structure of courses within PreseMaker, covering how courses, modules, and topics are organized in relation to one another. It also addresses how PreseMaker handles file storage, emphasizing that the application does not rely on proprietary or non-public storage systems. The video outlines the structure of generated artifacts produced by the application.

Relevance to Module: This video directly aligns with the module's focus on understanding the Course-Module-Content hierarchy in PreseMaker and how generated materials are stored on the user's local computer. It provides a walkthrough of the same structural concepts and file storage principles covered in this lesson, making it a natural companion resource.

Pedagogical Purpose

This video serves as a primary instructional resource and alternate explanation that reinforces MO1 and MO2 by visually presenting the four levels of the PreseMaker hierarchy and the parent-child relationships that connect them. It also supports MO3 by introducing the types of generated artifacts and the structural elements behind their production. Additionally, its emphasis on non-proprietary local storage grounds learners in the practical file ownership concepts needed for MO4 and MO5.
