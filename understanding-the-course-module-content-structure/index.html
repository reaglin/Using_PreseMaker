Understanding the Course-Module-Content Structure

This module introduces the organisational model that underpins PreseMaker. You will explore how courses, modules, topics, and generated content relate to one another, learn how PreseMaker stores files locally on your computer, and understand what it means to fully own the materials you create. Work through the resources below in order, or jump directly to any artifact you need.
