Generating Module Topics and Subtopics — Module Topics

Introduction to Module Topics and Subtopics

Overview of what module topics and subtopics are and why they serve as the foundational structure for all e-learning content. Establishes the purpose and importance of getting this structure right from the start.
  • What Are Module Topics? — Module topics are the major subject areas or learning units that make up an e-learning module.
  • What Are Subtopics? — Subtopics break each module topic down into smaller, more focused concepts or steps.
  • Why Topics and Subtopics Are the Foundational Structure — The topics-and-subtopics hierarchy is the architectural backbone upon which all other e-learning content is built.
  • The Purpose of Getting the Structure Right Early — Establishing an accurate topic and subtopic structure at the outset prevents costly rework in later stages of development.
  • How PreseMaker Generates Topics and Subtopics — PreseMaker analyzes a module's source materials to automatically produce a structured list of topics and subtopics.

How PreseMaker Analyzes Source Materials

Explains the process PreseMaker uses to examine uploaded or provided source materials and extract meaningful themes. Covers the logic behind how raw content is interpreted and converted into a structured topic list.
  • Ingesting Uploaded or Provided Source Materials — PreseMaker begins its analysis by reading all source materials supplied for the module, whether uploaded files or directly provided text.
  • Identifying Key Themes and Concepts — Once the source material is ingested, PreseMaker scans for recurring ideas, central arguments, and distinct subject areas that represent meaningful themes.
  • Converting Raw Content into Structured Topics — Identified themes are transformed from unstructured source material into discrete, learner-facing topic entries that form the Module Topics list.
  • Inferring Instructional Intent from the Source — PreseMaker does not simply extract words — it interprets the instructional purpose behind the source material to ensure topics align with learning goals.
  • Producing the Initial Topic List for Review — The output of PreseMaker's analysis is a draft Module Topics list that learners and authors can browse, evaluate, and edit before proceeding.

Browsing and Reviewing Generated Topics

Describes how to navigate and evaluate the Module Topics list once it has been generated. Learners explore how to assess whether the topics accurately reflect the intended scope and learning goals.
  • Locating the Module Topics List — After generation completes, the Module Topics list appears as the central workspace for reviewing your module structure.
  • Reading and Interpreting Topic Titles — Topic titles summarize the core idea PreseMaker extracted from the source content, giving you a quick snapshot of the proposed module structure.
  • Evaluating Coverage Against Learning Goals — A critical review step is comparing the generated topics against the intended scope and learning objectives of the module.
  • Assessing Topic Sequence and Logic — The order of topics affects how learners build understanding, so evaluating the sequence is as important as evaluating the content of individual topics.
  • Checking Topic Granularity — Granularity refers to how finely or broadly each topic is scoped, and getting this right ensures topics are teachable in a single focused segment.
  • Making Notes for Edits Before Proceeding — Before making changes, it is good practice to note all required adjustments so edits can be made systematically rather than piecemeal.

Editing and Refining Topics and Subtopics

Covers the tools and techniques available for modifying, reordering, adding, or removing topics and subtopics. Emphasizes best practices for ensuring the structure is accurate before proceeding to later steps.
  • Reviewing the Generated Topics List — Before making any edits, it is important to carefully review the full list of topics and subtopics that PreseMaker has generated from your source materials.
  • Renaming Topics and Subtopics — PreseMaker allows you to rename any topic or subtopic to better reflect its actual content or to match your instructional language.
  • Reordering Topics and Subtopics — The sequence of topics and subtopics shapes the learning journey, and PreseMaker provides tools to drag or move items into a more logical order.
  • Adding New Topics or Subtopics — If the generated list is missing important content areas, you can manually add new topics or subtopics to fill those gaps.
  • Removing Unnecessary Topics or Subtopics — Removing redundant or out-of-scope items keeps the module focused and prevents learners from encountering irrelevant content.
  • Best Practices for Finalizing the Structure — Getting the topic structure right before moving to later steps is critical, because subsequent content generation in PreseMaker is built directly on this foundation.

  • Topic Structure and Module Quality — A well-structured topic list directly improves the coherence, flow, and instructional quality of the completed module.
