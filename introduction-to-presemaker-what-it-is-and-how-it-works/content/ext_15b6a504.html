Author / Site: Dr. Ron Eaglin's Web and Database Programming

Summary: This video serves as the first in a 22-part series introducing learners to the PreseMaker tool, a Windows-based AI-assisted course authoring application available on the Windows App Store. It orients users to what PreseMaker is, who it is intended for, and provides a foundational overview of how the tool works. As the opening module of the series, it establishes the context and conceptual groundwork for all subsequent lessons.

Relevance to Module: This video directly aligns with the module's focus on introducing PreseMaker as an AI-assisted course authoring tool, covering the tool's purpose, its intended users, and its general operation. It provides learners with an authoritative first look at the software within the context of a structured, multi-part instructional series, making it a natural entry point for the module.

Pedagogical Purpose

This video provides a primary source introduction to PreseMaker from an experienced instructor, offering learners an authentic orientation to the tool that supports MO1 by helping students identify who PreseMaker is designed for and what authoring challenges it addresses. It also lays the conceptual groundwork for MO2 and MO3 by beginning to frame how the tool is structured and how it is used, giving students an initial mental model of the Course → Module → Content hierarchy and the authoring workflow before deeper exploration. Additionally, the introductory framing of AI assistance within the tool supports MO4 by prompting students to begin thinking critically about the role AI plays versus what the author must contribute.
